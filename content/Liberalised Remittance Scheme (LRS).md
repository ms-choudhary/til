By virtue of LRS scheme by RBI, a citizen of india can remit (send money) $2,50,000 (2.3 Crore) to a foreign entity per financial year. 

The purpose of this remittance can be:
- Investment, stocks or us treasury bonds
- Real estate investment
- Traveling abroad expenditure (business as well as leisure)
- Traveling for education
- Sending money to family overseas

Cost of transaction: money exchange cost (as facilitated by bank) + 18% GST on the exchange cost. 

TCS is applicable on this over limit of 10L. 
### For investments
Under International Financial Services Centres Authority (IFSCA), Global Access Provider (GAP) in GIFT city act as India's global investing gateway.
