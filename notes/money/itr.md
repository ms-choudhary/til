## Income Tax Return (ITR)

### Important Terms
- FY: Financial Year. April to April cycle in which government functions
- AY: Assessment Year. Starts after FY has ended. Tax is assessed on previous FY. For e.g., AY 2018-2019 assess the tax collection in FY 2017-2018.
- Form16: Provided by employer. Shows taxable income (after deductions from HRA exemption, 80C, 80D). Tax deducted at source (TDS) for each quarter. Whether you'll get refund or not.
- Form26AS: Employer gives to IT (Government) showcasing total salary provided & TDS deducted by employer.
### Important Dates
- Feb 15. Few months before FY end. Provide investment proof of investment declaration.
- April 1. Investment declaration. Standard. 1.5 lacs in 80C. Medical Insurance in 80D.
- July 31. File Income Tax Return.
### e-filing returns
- User name is your pan card no.
- ITR-1 is for individual
- It's quite simple. It's just tallying figures with your Form16. IT cell already has Form26as, which means they already know your total income, total tax paid by you as TDS.
- Income in ITR is not total income, but rather " Income chargeable under the head 'salaries'" in your Form16
- Sum all investments in each category. For e.g., for 80C, add ELSS + PF
- If you've switched jobs, you'll get Form16 from both employers. In such case, you've to sum income from both form16 and enter & likewise for other fields as well.
- Add all associated bank accounts
- Mention any additional income.
