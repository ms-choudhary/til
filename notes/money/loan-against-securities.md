# Loan Against Securities

You can take loan against stocks/mutual funds via Zerodha Capital, at rate of interest 11% (for loan amounts 25K to 50L). 

This covers most mutual funds as [listed here](https://zerodhacapital.com/approved-list).  

Haircut of 55% is applicable for equity based funds. For eg, if you assets are valued at 50 L, you can take a loan of only 22.5L on the remaining 45% after removing haircut.
